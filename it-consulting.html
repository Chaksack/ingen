FAQ

Common questions about IT consulting

We work with organizations of all sizes, from startups to Fortune 500 companies, across various industries including healthcare, finance, retail, manufacturing, and technology. Our consulting approach is tailored to each client's unique needs, challenges, and growth objectives.

Engagements vary based on scope and complexity. Strategic assessments may take 4-8 weeks, while digital transformation projects typically span 6-18 months. We offer flexible engagement models including project-based, retainer, and staff augmentation to meet your needs.

We offer both strategic consulting and hands-on implementation support. Our consultants can develop the strategy and roadmap, guide your internal teams through execution, or lead the implementation directly. We adapt our involvement level based on your team's capabilities and preferences.

We take a comprehensive approach including risk assessments, security architecture design, policy development, employee training, and incident response planning. We follow frameworks like NIST, ISO 27001, and CIS Controls to ensure robust protection against evolving threats.

Our consultants hold industry certifications including PMP, TOGAF, CISSP, AWS/Azure/GCP certifications, and ITIL. They average 10+ years of experience in IT leadership roles and have successfully delivered hundreds of projects across diverse industries and technologies.
